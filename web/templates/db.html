Each round deals a "ticket bag": every session gets tickets according to its score (at least 1 — nothing is ever left out), the bag is shuffled, and the frame draws one per cycle without replacement until it is empty. So a high score shows up more often without hogging the turn or locking the frame into a loop between two sessions — none can repeat more times than its number of tickets before the others have had their turn.
